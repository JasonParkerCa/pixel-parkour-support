Privacy Policy

Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version

Pixel Parkour doesn't collect your personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share data. The few features that go online are provided by Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts of its own, doesn't ask for your name, email or location, and doesn't send information about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best score, hearts, unlocked characters, chosen character and settings (music, sound and language) on your device so you can pick up where you left off.

Apple services the game uses

  • iCloud: if you're signed in to iCloud, your best score, unused hearts, free unlocks, earned characters and chosen character are stored in your own iCloud account so they sync across your devices. We can't see this data. You can delete it in your device's iCloud settings; doing so removes unused hearts and characters earned at 1,000m.
  • Game Center: if you're signed in, your best score is sent to the Game Center leaderboard and the game shows your friends' scores. Game Center is run by Apple and shows your Game Center nickname, not your real name, unless you chose to use it.
  • In-app purchases: purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The game checks with the App Store which characters you own so it can restore them.

Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Pixel Parkour is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. In-app purchases can be managed with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings.

Emails to support

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and never share them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
