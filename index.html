Playing

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to jump. Tap again while in the air to jump higher. You can jump up to three times before you land. Run from rooftop to rooftop and avoid antennas, power lines, drones, birds, steam, stones and UFO lasers. To practise every obstacle, tap Tutorial on the title screen.

Can I play with a keyboard on Mac or iPad?

Yes. Press Space to jump, start a run or retry, and Escape to go back or close a page. In the Store, the left and right arrow keys pick the previous or next character.

What are the zones?

The city changes every 500m: Downtown, Construction Site, UFO Invasion and Storm, then it loops. Each zone brings its own hazards, and every 500m you get a long, safe rooftop to take a breather.

What are hearts for?

When you crash, you can spend hearts to revive and keep your run going. Each revive in the same run costs one more heart than the last. Collect hearts on rooftops while you run, or get them in the Store. Hearts you buy are saved for later.

How do I unlock characters?
  • Six characters are free from the start.
  • Reach 1,000m in a single run to earn a free unlock, then spend it on any locked character in the Store.
  • You can also unlock a character in the Store with an in-app purchase.

Each unlockable character has a special skill, shown in the Store when you pick them.

Purchases

My purchased character is missing

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Characters you buy are linked to your Apple Account, so they come back on any of your devices.

My purchase is waiting for approval

If Ask to Buy is on for your account, a family organizer needs to approve the purchase. Once it's approved, the item is added to the game automatically.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

What are the Mystery Box odds?

The Store shows the odds next to the Mystery Box before you buy it. Every box contains hearts within the range shown on its button.

Saves and settings

Is my progress saved across my devices?

Yes, when you're signed in to iCloud. Your best score, unused hearts, free unlocks and characters earned at 1,000m sync across your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Changes usually arrive within a minute or two.

Important: if you delete this game's data from iCloud, unused hearts and characters earned at 1,000m are removed. Characters you bought can always be brought back with Restore Purchases.

How do I change the language or volume?

Open Settings on the title screen. Drag the Music and Sound sliders to set the volume, or tap them to switch sound on or off. Tap Language to choose a language, or pick Auto to follow your device. The game is available in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

The leaderboard doesn't open

The leaderboard uses Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings app, then open the game again.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You can play offline. An internet connection is only needed for the Store, the leaderboard and syncing with iCloud.
